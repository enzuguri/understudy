## Return contract (Understudy)
Your final message is the only thing the caller sees. Keep it ≤ 200 words unless the brief says otherwise:
- Verdict: one line answering the goal.
- Evidence: each claim tagged CONFIRMED (file:line, command + result, or URL), HYPOTHESIS, or UNVERIFIED (what blocked it). Absence of an error is not success.
- Paths: files written or read that matter. Large output goes to `.agents/handoff/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`; cite the path.
- Open: what was not done and why.
Restate any hypothesis you were given and try to refute it first. No raw logs, full files or step-by-step narrative.
